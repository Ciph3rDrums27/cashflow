Composition this month

Credit card balances

Enter each card's balance, APR, and the payment you make to that card. The summary ranks them by APR and shows a real payoff per card; the total payment feeds your cash outflow up top.

Sections: Affirm and Credit-card plans are installments — anchored to the date you enter and counted forward for the payments left, so future months are exact and a plan drops off the month after its last payment. Credit-card charges (payments field blank) are ongoing every month. Recurring bills can be Fixed or "Varies monthly" (an estimate for forecasts plus a per-month actual). To avoid double-counting, don't list the same dollars in two sections. Backup: Export JSON saves a file of everything; Import JSON restores it.
